A measurement period starts with its first recompute pass and closes on the first pass more than one second later. One high period arms the warning. A second consecutive high period emits it.

hx-live warns once per high-load period. Stop and reset removes every binding, deactivates hx-live, and clears that warning state.

What each scenario does

  • One long pass: 200 bindings do about 0.12ms each, once. Expect an immediate warning.
  • Heavy binding jank: 200 bindings do about 0.15ms each, every frame. Expect an immediate warning and visible frame loss.
  • Sustained work: 50 bindings do about 0.05ms each, every frame. Each pass stays below 16ms, but total work exceeds 100ms per period. Expect a warning after about two seconds.

Timings vary by browser and machine. Reload if a preset lands close to a threshold.
